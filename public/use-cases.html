The Challenge

Why enrollment marketing stalls

Education buyers research for weeks, compare outcomes, and need reassurance before they commit. Most institutes lose students to slow follow-up and generic messaging rather than to a better competitor.

Long, multi-touch cycles

A single student may see 7–12 touchpoints across search, social, and email before enquiring. Manual teams drop the thread halfway.

Wasted, unqualified leads

Broad campaigns fill your CRM with prospects who never intended to enrol, inflating cost per admission.

Seasonal demand spikes

Intake windows concentrate demand into a few weeks. Budget that is not paced to the calendar burns out before the peak.
