Creating Ad Creatives

Follow these steps to create powerful ad creatives with AI.

1

Go to Ad Creatives

  • Log in to your digiAd AI account and open the Ad Creatives section.
  • Click Create New Ad Creative button to begin.
2

Name Your Creative

  • Give your ad a unique name to easily identify it later.
3

Select Platform

  • Choose the platforms you want to create ads for — Facebook, Google, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, X, or YouTube.
  • Select the relevant Ad Type for each platform.
4

Add Product or Service Details

  • Paste the product or service URL and let AI auto-fill the details for you.
  • Or, enter the information manually if you prefer.
  • Click Fetch Details, then proceed by clicking Next.
5

Generate Ad Copies

  • Sit back and let digiAd AI creates multiple ad copies tailored to your brand and audience — in minutes.
6

Choose a Call to Action

  • Select the most suitable Call to Action from the dropdown.
  • Click Save to store your creative for later, or Save & Launch to publish your ad right away.
