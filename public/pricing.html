Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about digiAd AI and how it can help transform your digital marketing.

Q1. Do all plans include every digiAd AI feature?+
Yes! Every single plan—from Launch to Lead—includes the complete digiAd AI platform with unlimited copy generation, competitor research, campaign launches, social posting, and editors. Only your monthly allocation of generation credits changes.
Q2. What are credits?+
Credits are the universal currency used on digiAd AI to power compute-intensive AI operations like generating photorealistic ad images, high-retention video creatives, UGC shorts, CGI animations, and AI avatar presentations.
Q3. How are credits deducted when generating advertising videos?+
Credits are deducted per completed generation based on the creative asset type and resolution. For example, standard AI ad images consume fewer credits, while full motion AI UGC video creatives and realistic avatar renders consume more due to higher GPU rendering requirements.
Q4. What happens if I run out of credits?+
You never have to pause your active campaigns! All your live campaigns continue running normally. To generate additional creatives, you can easily top up credits through our Pay As You Go Add-on widget or upgrade your monthly tier instantly.
Q5. Do unused credits expire?+
No! With our 50% Credit Rollover policy, 50% of any unused credits from your active monthly billing cycle automatically roll over to the following month, protecting your investment.
Q6. Is there a free trial?+
Yes. You can explore the platform completely risk-free with our Explore Free plan which includes 100 free credits and unlimited social posting, with no credit card required.
Q7. How does the Free plan work?+
The Free plan is Free Forever. You get 100 complimentary credits to test our creative generation engines, unlimited social publishing across your channels, unlimited campaign launches with your own assets, daily analytics emails, and 10 competitor ad audits every month via AdLens.
Q8. What is Pay As You Go?+
Pay As You Go allows you to purchase credits on demand without committing to a recurring monthly subscription. It is ideal for seasonal product launches, flash sales, or one-off creative testing.
Q9. Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my subscription?+
Yes, you have complete flexibility. You can upgrade or downgrade between Launch, Grow, Scale, and Lead at any time from your account dashboard. Upgrades are pro-rated, and you can cancel anytime with a single click.
Q10. Which plan should I choose?+
If you are starting out or running 1-2 small campaigns, Launch (300 credits) is great. If you are an active brand or e-commerce merchant testing creatives weekly, Grow (1,000 credits) is our most popular choice. High-velocity brands should select Scale (2,000 credits), while agencies and fast-scaling enterprises pick Lead (5,000 credits).
Q11. Are there any feature limitations on lower plans?+
None whatsoever. Unlike other platforms that lock advanced algorithms behind expensive enterprise plans, every paying digiAd AI customer gets full access to our complete AI tool suite, multi-channel publisher, competitor spy tools, and watermark-free exports.
Q12. Is my advertising budget included in the subscription?+
No. Your subscription pays for the digiAd AI software platform and creative generation credits. Your media ad spend is paid directly from your own connected ad accounts to Google, Meta, TikTok, or LinkedIn.
Q13. Can I connect my own advertising accounts?+
Yes! You can connect your existing Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), TikTok, and LinkedIn accounts via official 1-click OAuth APIs. You retain 100% control, ownership, and direct billing with the networks.
Q14. How is digiAd AI different from other AI marketing platforms?+
Most tools only write text or create images. digiAd AI is a full-funnel autonomous advertising platform: it plans your strategy with predictive ROAS, researches competitor winning hooks, renders photorealistic multi-format UGC and CGI video ads, publishes directly across channels, and protects your spend with real-time autonomous bid and budget rebalancing.
